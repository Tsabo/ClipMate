# What's new since ClipMate 7

This ClipMate is a new program, built to work the way ClipMate 7 does. It's free and open source. It also adds:

- **Your clips across your computers.** Pair your own computers, and each can browse, search and copy the clips the others share. See [Use ClipMate across your Devices](../devices/index.md).
- **Light and dark themes.** Choose one on the [Appearance](../options/visual.md) tab.
- **A better text editor.** Clips open in an editor with line numbers and syntax highlighting. See the [Text tab](../user-interface/preview-edit/text-tab.md).
- **A Markdown tab.** Clips copied from web pages, Word and other rich-text programs can be viewed as Markdown.
- **Search across every database.** **All Databases** in **Search in** searches all of them at once, your Devices' shared ones included. See [Search](../user-interface/main-toolbar/search.md).
- **Troubleshooting tools.** Clipboard Diagnostics shows what a program really put on the clipboard, and Paste Trace shows what happened during a paste.
