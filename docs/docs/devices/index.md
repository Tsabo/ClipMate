# Use ClipMate across your Devices

If you run ClipMate on more than one computer, they can work together. Each copy of ClipMate is a Device. Once you've paired two of your Devices, each can browse, search and copy the clips the other chooses to share. A Peer is another of your Devices that you've paired with this one.

Your clips aren't sent to any online service. Devices talk directly to each other, and each one's clips stay on it unless you copy them.

1. [Pair your Devices](pair.md) so they trust each other.
2. [Share and search databases](share-and-search.md) to see a Device's clips from another.
3. [Copy clips to local](copy-to-local.md) to keep the ones you want.

Networking is off until you turn it on. While it's off, ClipMate opens no network port and sends no network traffic. All the settings are described in [Devices Options](../options/devices.md).
